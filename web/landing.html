JOB 02

Review
your paper

You upload your draft. It extracts your claims, finds the surrounding literature, and runs three independent reviewers who attack your framing, your method, and your significance. Each reviewer runs on a different AI model family, so they don't share blind spots.

Framing — “novel” overstates prior art MAJOR
Method — no correction for multiple tests MODERATE
Significance — effect size within noise MINOR
EVERY OBJECTION SHIPS WITH ITS MINIMUM FIX
THE DIFFERENTIATOR

Seven model lineages, in adjudicated, opposed roles.

Not one model answering. Reviewers are drawn from separate model families so their blind spots don't overlap, then an adjudicator weighs what survives contact with the others.

R1 / FRAMING

Attacks the question you chose to ask and the prior work you left out of it.

R2 / METHOD

Attacks design, sample, controls, and the statistics you used to close the gap.

R3 / SIGNIFICANCE

Attacks the claim that it matters, and whether your novelty claim survives a referee.

THE RUN

Ninety seconds you can watch.

A full run takes up to about ninety seconds. Named stages report as they complete, and warnings surface without stopping the work.

Every claim traces back to a specific paper.
